1. Local data

Settings, prompt templates, trial state, and translation history stay on your device. API keys use secure system storage, not ordinary settings, history, or diagnostic logs. Prompt previews do not create history entries.

PopTrans has no ads, trackers, or developer analytics service. No developer server receives your translation content or keys.

2. Cloud requests

Text, languages, and prompts are sent only when you request it and permit the selected service to receive data. The default is Apple Private Cloud Compute, without a third-party API key. Apple states that PCC does not retain request content or results and Apple cannot access them. Limited metadata, such as request size and duration, may be collected without content or account linkage.

You may instead select OpenAI or Gemini using your own API key. Requests go directly to that service with text, prompts, and model settings. It may also receive your IP address, approximate region, account, and usage information. Use and retention depend on its policy, plan, and account settings; processing may occur in other countries or regions.

Network requests use encrypted connections. Services receiving data must provide protection equivalent to this policy and applicable requirements. You must also meet the service’s age requirements.

  • Apple Intelligence & Privacy
  • OpenAI data controls
  • Gemini API Additional Terms

Withdraw permission for the selected service in Settings to stop future requests. Switching services does not authorize another service, and withdrawing permission does not delete saved keys.

Do not send confidential, sensitive, or personal information you are not authorized to provide.

3. Device features

Selection translation receives only text supplied by the source app through system Services. PopTrans does not request Accessibility permission, read other apps’ interfaces, simulate copying, or monitor global keystrokes.

Source-text speech runs on your device without sending text or audio to AI providers.

4. Purchases and deletion

Purchases and restores are handled by the app’s distribution platform. The operator does not receive payment card information.

Local data remains until you delete history, reset settings, or remove the corresponding storage. You can remove keys separately in the app; uninstalling alone may not remove system-stored credentials.

For cloud data deletion, use the selected service’s account controls or contact it directly. The operator cannot access or delete that data on your behalf.
