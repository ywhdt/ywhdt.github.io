Calories & Nutrition Made Easy

CalPace helps you track food and nutrient intake. Everyday logging works offline, with no CalPace account required.

  • Meal logging: Enter calories and nutrients manually, or add foods from the library. Edit, copy, save drafts and review past entries.
  • Nutrition targets: Set daily goals for calories, protein, carbs and fat.
  • Offline foods: Browse 19,000+ food references with source and cooking or processing information.
  • Meal templates: Save regular meals and reuse them, adjusting portions or nutrient values as needed.
  • Apple Health: With your permission, write nutrition data or read height and weight.

OPTIONAL AI ASSISTANT

On eligible devices, Apple Private Cloud Compute (PCC) is used by default to help log and edit meals with text or photos, with no API key required. You can review entries, undo the assistant's latest changes and connect your own AI service.

AI assistance requires iOS 27 or later and a compatible device with PCC support. AI features are not available in all regions and are subject to service availability and usage limits.

Meals, templates, plans and drafts are saved on your device. AI submissions are sent to your selected provider with your consent.
