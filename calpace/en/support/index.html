Entries and food references

Open Entries → Quick entry to enter nutrients manually or add foods from the library. Food lookup works offline. Source, cooking state and processing affect nutrient values. Recipe estimates are references, not measurements of every restaurant or preparation.

Trial, purchase and restore

Start the seven-day trial yourself through the App Store. It runs continuously from the original start, with no automatic renewal or charge. A one-time purchase unlocks adding and editing. When access expires, existing entries remain viewable.

Use Settings → Access & purchases → Restore purchases with the original App Store account. You do not need a CalPace account. Restoration does not recover local meals or restart the trial. Starting the trial, purchasing and restoring require a connection; everyday logging and food lookup work offline with valid access.

Templates, languages and Apple Health

Save regular meals as templates and adjust them before reuse. The app supports Simplified Chinese, Traditional Chinese, English, Japanese, Korean, German, French and Spanish. Settings → App language opens system settings. Existing meal names and nutrient values are preserved.

Apple Health is optional and requires permission; manual logging works without it. Height and weight are read only when requested and authorized. Turning off sync retains previously written samples. The app can remove the Health data it wrote without touching another app’s data.

Optional AI assistant

AI requires iOS 27 or later, a compatible device with PCC support, the PCC service ready and valid app access. AI is not available in all regions. Apple Private Cloud Compute (PCC) is selected by default, with no API key required. Text and photos are supported. PCC does not provide web search. There is no automatic fallback to another provider.

Before the first submission, review the recipient and data and consent to sending. You may connect your own AI service in Settings → Configure assistant. CalPace does not sell API keys or usage credits. Third-party provider terms, fees and usage limits apply. The assistant can review entries and undo its recent changes. Check results before relying on them.

Unavailable or exhausted services may prevent AI requests. Manual records remain available with valid access. You can cancel requests and retain drafts for retry.

Local data

The app requires iOS 26 or later. Meals, templates, plans, drafts and conversations are stored on the device. Restoring purchases does not restore those files. Keep any records you need before changing devices or reinstalling.

Settings let you manage or delete local data; deletion cannot be undone. API keys are removed separately in assistant settings. Review the privacy policy for attachments, Health data and provider processing.
