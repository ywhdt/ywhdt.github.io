Privacy policy

wentianzh.apps@outlook.com

Updated October 3, 2026. Developer: ZHANG WENTIAN.

On-device data

Meals, templates, plans, body information, drafts, conversations and assistant preferences are stored on your device. No CalPace account is required. The app does not provide meal cloud sync, sell personal information or use it for advertising tracking. Your own API keys are stored in the system Keychain, outside meal and conversation files, and do not migrate to another device through backup.

AI and network access

Opening a conversation or switching providers does not send data. AI is offered only when system, device, region and service conditions allow. Apple Private Cloud Compute (PCC) is the default and needs no API key. You may connect your own Gemini or OpenAI service. Before the first submission, the app explains the recipient and content and asks for consent. It does not automatically send input to another provider when a service is unavailable or exhausted.

Text you submit, relevant conversation context and attachments are processed by the selected provider. To carry out a request, the assistant may read and send relevant meals, templates, plans, saved body fields and preferences. It does not automatically upload your entire history or Apple Health data. Provider retention and processing follow that provider’s terms; review them before configuring your own service.

PCC supports text and photos without web search. Supported search may be used by default for a task; queries and related context are processed by the applicable service. Existing personal settings remain. You can cancel requests, disable the assistant or clear its data.

Storefront information comes from StoreKit, not language, location or IP, and is not persistently cached. Unavailable storefront information does not add a restriction; device and PCC requirements still apply. Offline mode does not offer AI or other online app services. App Store downloads, transactions, storefront reads and restoration remain handled by Apple.

Photos

Camera permission is requested when you take a photo; the photo picker reads only selected photos. Adding an attachment does not send it. Images are reprocessed before submission to remove metadata such as location.

Unsent or retryable attachments stay on the device. When photo retention is enabled, photos from the current and previous conversation are retained. Disabling it removes processed photos. Clearing conversations removes associated attachments.

Food lookup and sources

Offline food lookup does not send search terms. Reference data comes from USDA, Japan’s MEXT, Taiwan’s TFDA, ANSES Ciqual and UK CoFID. CalPace also provides labeled reference recipe estimates. The app displays sources, licenses, processing states and original nutrient definitions. Translated names do not alter nutrient values.

When the assistant looks up a packaged product, that lookup sends only its barcode to Open Food Facts; images are not submitted to that lookup endpoint.

Apple Health

With permission, the app writes meal nutrients and syncs subsequent edits or deletion. Height and weight are read only when requested and authorized, and retained only when you save them. Saved body fields may be used in relevant AI requests. Turning off sync retains existing Health data. The app can remove the samples it wrote without touching another app’s data.

Purchases and deletion

Apple handles purchases and payment. CalPace verifies transactions and the original trial time locally to determine access. Restoration does not recover local meals or restart the trial.

Settings let you disable the assistant, clear conversations or delete local data. Deletion cannot be undone. API keys are removed separately in assistant settings. These controls remain available in view-only mode.

Feedback and website

Contact details and information you voluntarily send by email are used only to reply and troubleshoot, then deleted when no longer needed or on request. Never send keys or passwords. Apple processes TestFlight build, session, crash and submitted feedback information under its rules.

GitHub Pages hosts the website and GitHub may process access logs such as IP addresses. This site uses no advertising, analytics scripts or tracking cookies. Changes to this policy or expanded AI submissions are explained where applicable, with consent requested for new submission scope.
