iPhone · Free app · Version 1.3

Estimate from three sites.
Track changes over time.

Use the Jackson–Pollock three-site method to estimate body fat percentage. One valid reading per site is enough; add repeat readings to compare consistency, switch between millimeters and inches, and optionally add the final estimate to Apple Health.

Important

The app provides an estimate based on the values you enter. It is intended only for general fitness and personal tracking and is not medical advice, diagnosis, or treatment. Measurement technique, individual differences, and input errors can affect the result.
