Privacy Policy

Effective date: August 30, 2026 · Applies to version 1.3 and later

Skinfold does not require an account. Calculations and local history stay on your device. You can choose to add one final body fat percentage estimate to Apple Health; the developer does not collect data or track you.

This policy applies to the Skinfold iOS app provided by WENTIAN ZHANG.

1. How data is processed

To estimate body fat percentage, the app processes the sex you select and the age and readings from three skinfold sites you enter on your device. It then generates a body fat percentage, reference category, and any retake guidance. The app writes the sex, age, sum of the three site averages, body fat percentage, category, and measurement date to local history only when you choose Save to history. Individual raw readings are not saved.

2. Optional Apple Health write

After a calculation, you can explicitly choose Add to Apple Health. Skinfold requests write permission only for body fat percentage and does not request or read HealthKit data. If you allow the write, iOS stores the final estimate, measurement time, and Skinfold as the source in HealthKit. Skinfold does not write your sex, age, reference category, local history, or individual skinfold readings, and it does not automatically sync or backfill earlier results.

3. Data collection and sharing

Skinfold does not send your entries, estimates, local history, or HealthKit data to the developer, advertisers, analytics providers, or other third-party services. The app has no accounts, ads, analytics, developer-operated cloud sync, or remote service, and it does not track you. Apple Health is an Apple system service. Depending on your Apple and iCloud settings, Apple may store or sync HealthKit data between your devices; the developer cannot access that data. Skinfold does not use health data for advertising, marketing, or data mining.

4. Manage and delete data

You can delete an individual local record on the History page, or choose Clear history to delete all local history. Local history is excluded from system backups; deleting the app removes the remaining local history.

Local history and Apple Health samples are separate. Deleting one does not delete the other. Use the Health app to view or delete a sample, and use Apple Health or iOS Settings to manage Skinfold's permission. Deleting Skinfold does not automatically remove samples already added to Apple Health.

5. Website

This Privacy Policy and the support pages are static pages with no ads or analytics scripts. They are hosted on GitHub Pages, so GitHub may process network request information needed to provide and protect that service. Measurement data from the app is not sent to this website or to GitHub.

6. Updates and contact

If the way data is processed changes materially, the developer will update this policy, its effective date, and the App Store privacy information.

Developer: WENTIAN ZHANG. Email ywhdt_zh@outlook.com, or report an issue through GitHub Issues. Do not include unnecessary personal health information in an email or public issue.
