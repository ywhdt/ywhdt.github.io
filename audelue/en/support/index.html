Get started

  1. Open Audelue on macOS 26 or later.
  2. Choose transcription, translated transcription, or speech translation, then an engine and languages.
  3. Select system mix, a specific app, or the microphone, and grant the requested system permissions. System mix may include sound from other apps.
  4. Start the session. Pause, resume, or finish as needed; adjust caption order, size, and lines in Settings.

Apple on-device transcription is a good starting point. Some languages need system resources to be downloaded. Cloud engines need separate configuration.

Connect a cloud engine

In Settings → Cloud & Privacy, select a service, allow processing by that service, and save your own API key. Microsoft Azure also requires a Speech endpoint that matches your resource. You can then use Test Connection.

A successful check confirms connectivity and authentication, not access to every model, region, or quota. Provider charges are separate from the Audelue full-version purchase.

Adjust bilingual and Live captions

In caption settings, choose whether source text or translation comes first and set each track’s line count. Automatic lines default to one for Chinese and Japanese and two for Latin text. You can also choose one to four lines.

Engines marked Live provide incremental captions. Other routes wait for each audio segment to be processed. Translations may arrive later than source text; the two tracks advance independently.

No audio or captions

Check that your source is playing, the session is not paused, and the source language is correct. Check microphone or screen and system audio recording permissions in macOS System Settings → Privacy & Security. You may need to reopen the app after changing a permission.

A system permission may mention screen recording. Audelue’s production capture processes audio only and does not save screen video. If one app produces no audio, try an ordinary local source; some apps and protected content may not be capturable.

Free sessions, purchases, and restore

Free sessions allow up to three minutes of running time. Paused time does not count. At the limit, Audelue finishes processing accepted content and keeps the session window available so you can view captions or history.

The full version only removes the session time limit. After release, purchase or restore it in Settings → General → Full Version. Cancelling does not unlock the app; pending purchases unlock after approval. The product is not publicly available yet, so an unavailable purchase is currently expected.

Family Sharing is planned for the released product, subject to its App Store availability and your family settings. If a purchase later fails to restore, check that you use the purchasing account and select Restore Purchases in the app.

Manage history and privacy

Turning history saving off affects future sessions and keeps existing records. Export or delete individual sessions, or clear history, in the history window. Original audio is not saved as history. Delete exported files separately from the location you chose.

In Cloud & Privacy, withdraw a service’s processing consent, delete its key, or clear saved prompt data. Finish a session before deleting a key it is using. Withdrawing consent does not delete information already received by a provider. See the privacy policy.

Contact support

Email ywhdt_zh@outlook.com with the app version or build number, macOS version, audio source, engine and languages, steps to reproduce, and error message.

Do not send API keys, complete purchase receipts, or private recordings and captions. Redact sensitive content in screenshots. Emails are used to handle your support request; you can also contact this address for privacy or deletion requests.
