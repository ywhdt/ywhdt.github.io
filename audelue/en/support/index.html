Get started

Requires an Apple silicon Mac with macOS 27 or later.

  1. Choose Local or Pro, then set the audio language and optional translation.
  2. Select system mix, a specific app, or the microphone, and grant the requested permissions.
  3. Select Start Captions. Pause, resume, or end the session as needed.

Local and Pro

Local mode transcribes and translates on your device for free. Language resources may need to download on first use.

Pro offers cloud transcription and optional Real-time or Quality translation, sharing one time balance. The app asks for cloud processing consent before starting when needed.

Captions and languages

Adjust Cinematic or Teleprompter appearance in Settings → Subtitles. Cinematic has a bottom-center shortcut. Move or resize the teleprompter and adjust its background and opacity.

Choose and order quick-menu languages in Settings → General → Favorite Languages.

No audio or captions

Check that audio is playing, the session is not paused, and the language is correct. Check audio recording or microphone permissions in macOS System Settings → Privacy & Security. Reopen the app after changing permissions if needed.

For Pro, also check your network and balance. Some apps or protected content may not be capturable; try a local audio file to check.

Time and restore

New users can claim one 20-minute trial. Pro Time offers 1-, 5-, and 10-hour packs at the prices shown in the app. Purchased time does not expire or renew automatically.

Pro deducts running time, with no extra time charge for translation. Silence and normal processing waits still count. Pausing Audelue, sleep, work after stopping, and confirmed service interruptions do not count. Pausing only the player does not pause the timer.

After changing Macs or reinstalling, use the original App Store account and select Restore Purchases and Balance in Pro Time. If asked to restore device authorization, select Restore this Mac’s access and follow the prompts.

The session ends when your balance runs out, without buying more time. Local mode remains available.

History and privacy

View, export, or delete history on your Mac; it is not automatically synced to the cloud. Turning saving off keeps existing history. Manage exported files separately.

Withdraw cloud processing consent in Settings → Cloud & Privacy. See the privacy policy for data handling and deletion.

Contact support

Email wentianzh.apps@outlook.com with your app and macOS versions, audio source and languages, steps, and error message. Do not send passwords, keys, private recordings, or captions. Redact sensitive details in screenshots.
