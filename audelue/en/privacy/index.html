Audelue Privacy Policy

This policy explains how Audelue handles audio, captions, purchase information and other related data.

1. Audio and local history

Audelue processes your selected system, app or microphone audio after you start a session and grant permission, for transcription and optional translation. Screen recording permission is used only to capture audio, not screen video. Apple local mode processes audio and captions on your device without sending them to the cloud.

The app does not save recordings. Settings, keywords and translation preferences stay on your device. With history enabled, captions and related session information are kept until you delete them, for viewing and export. History is not automatically synced to the cloud.

2. When you use cloud Pro

When you use Pro, your device sends audio and relevant information such as languages and keywords directly to Soniox for transcription and optional real-time translation. Quality translation sends the relevant source text, necessary context and translation preferences through Audelue’s servers to DeepSeek, without audio.

Audio does not pass through Audelue’s servers. Quality translation text is used only for immediate processing and is not saved on the server. Cloud connections are encrypted, and Audelue’s servers are hosted by Microsoft Azure.

Cloud processing may take place outside your country or region. Providers have their own policies for handling and retaining received data. Please review them before enabling cloud processing: Soniox · DeepSeek.

3. Cloud balance and purchases

While providing Pro services, we keep necessary purchase, usage, trial and device authorization information to manage your balance, restore unused time, handle refunds and prevent duplicate credits. These records do not contain audio, captions or translation preference content.

Apple handles purchases. We do not receive payment card details or your Apple account email or password. Related data handling is covered by Apple’s privacy policy. We also use necessary service status and error information to maintain the service, without recording audio or caption content.

4. Your choices and deletion

You can pause or end a session, withdraw cloud processing consent, or use Settings to delete history, revoke device authorization or delete the cloud account. Withdrawing consent stops new cloud processing without deleting local history or remaining time. Turning history saving off does not delete existing history.

Deleting the cloud account ends cloud sessions and removes device links and authorization information. Local history must be deleted separately. Necessary records for balance restoration, refunds, duplicate prevention and account security remain. Old backup copies are removed within seven days.

Uninstalling the app does not delete cloud records. Manage exported files and device backups separately. Deleting local data does not remove data already sent to providers; contact us if you wish to request deletion.

5. Website and support

The app and website have no advertising or usage analytics. The website is hosted by GitHub Pages without tracking cookies. See GitHub’s privacy statement for its handling of visit information.

If you email support, the developer and email provider receive your address, message and attachments. These are kept only as needed to handle the issue or meet legal obligations. You can request deletion of support information.

6. Contact and updates

Audelue is intended for users aged 18 and older. The developer and operator is ZHANG WENTIAN. For privacy questions or requests to access, correct or delete relevant information, contact wentianzh.apps@outlook.com. Policy changes will be posted on this page.
