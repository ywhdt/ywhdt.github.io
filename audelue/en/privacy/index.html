Audelue Privacy Policy

Effective date: 2026-09-12

1. Scope

This policy covers Audelue for macOS and its website. The developer and operator is ZHANG WENTIAN, reachable at ywhdt_zh@outlook.com.

Audelue has no developer account system or developer-operated server receiving your audio, captions, or API keys. The app contains no advertising or third-party analytics SDK and does not automatically send usage analytics or crash reports to the developer. Cloud routes connect directly to the selected provider.

2. Audio and system permissions

After you start a session and grant the necessary permissions, Audelue processes your selected system, app, or microphone audio. System mix may include sound from multiple apps. Screen and system audio recording permission is used for audio capture, not to save screen video.

Original audio is temporarily held in memory for transcription, translation, and necessary processing or retries. Audelue does not save recordings and releases audio after the relevant processing or session ends. Apple on-device transcription and translation process content on the device; resource downloads, system diagnostics, or purchases may still contact Apple services.

3. Information stored on your Mac

Local settings include interface language, engines and languages, caption appearance, and processing consent. Context, keywords, translation preferences, and terminology you enter may be saved per model in ordinary local settings. These may contain sensitive information and are not secrets protected in Keychain.

When history is enabled, Audelue may save final source text and translations, times, languages, source type, models, processing state, necessary configuration, and provider-reported usage summaries. History excludes individual temporary caption revisions, prompt text, API keys, and original audio. History stays local and is not synced to other devices by the app.

API keys are stored in system Keychain, not in ordinary settings, history, or app logs. The app does not sync them to other devices. Local history and settings rely on macOS and your device protections, without additional app-level database encryption. System backups may include local data. Exported captions are subject to the storage location and sync settings you choose.

4. Cloud processing and recipients

Each cloud service requires separate processing consent and your own credentials. Cloud transcription and speech translation send necessary audio. Cloud text translation sends source text, limited preceding context, the target language, and instructions needed to generate a result. Depending on the route, requests may include your context, keywords, or translation preferences. When transcription and translation use different services, each receives the corresponding data. Manual translation retries from history also send the relevant text and necessary preceding context.

Test Connection sends metadata requests for connectivity and authentication, not audio, captions, or prompts. Saving a key alone does not run this check. Requests give the provider authentication information and expose IP address, request time, model, usage, and security metadata.

Providers may process information in other countries or regions and retain it for service operation, security, or legal requirements, depending on your plan, region, and settings. Content retention and training practices vary. For example, Gemini unpaid services may use inputs and outputs for product improvement and human review. Check the applicable terms before processing sensitive information and do not send it to an unsuitable service.

  • OpenAI — API data controls
  • Google — Gemini API terms
  • Microsoft — Speech data privacy
  • Soniox — Privacy Policy

Audelue does not promise that every cloud service avoids retention or training. Review each provider’s current policy and your account’s data controls before enabling it.

We require supported providers to offer the same or equivalent data protection as this policy and applicable requirements within the processing scope disclosed here. We review their published practices and will change or discontinue support if these requirements cannot be met.

5. Apple purchases and diagnostics

Apple handles purchases, restoration, and Family Sharing through the App Store and StoreKit. Audelue reads verified product and transaction entitlement information to determine full-version access and respond to refunds or sharing revocations. It does not receive your payment card details or upload transaction receipts to a developer server.

Depending on your system or TestFlight settings, Apple may collect diagnostics and testing feedback and make reports available to the developer. This is separate from app-managed uploads and is governed by Apple’s privacy policy and the relevant settings.

6. Your controls and deletion

  • Pause or finish a session to stop accepting more audio. Normal finish completes processing already accepted content.
  • Withdraw a service’s processing consent in Settings → Cloud & Privacy to stop further requests to it. A related active session is cancelled.
  • Delete keys or clear saved prompt data on the same page. Finish a session before deleting a key it is using.
  • Turn future history saving off, delete a session, or clear history. Manage exported files and system backups separately.

Withdrawing consent or deleting local records cannot recall information already sent. Use the provider’s account controls or privacy channels for its retained information. Uninstalling the app may not delete separately stored Keychain credentials, backups, or exports.

7. Website and support email

These are static GitHub Pages pages with no advertising, analytics scripts, tracking cookies, or online forms. GitHub may process IP addresses and request information to provide and protect hosting, as described in its privacy statement. Opening this website does not attach your app audio, captions, or keys.

If you email support, the developer and email service provider receive your email address, message, and attachments to respond and resolve the issue. Share only necessary information. Correspondence is kept as needed to handle the request and meet necessary legal obligations; you can contact us to request deletion.

8. Use and privacy requests

Only process audio you have the right to capture and send to the chosen service, and obtain participants’ consent where needed. Captions and translations can be wrong and do not replace professional judgment. Providers have their own age, regional, and account conditions.

Contact the email below to request access, correction, deletion, or other applicable privacy rights concerning support information held by the developer. Use the app’s deletion controls for data held only on your device; the developer cannot remotely read or recover those records.

9. Updates and contact

When processing practices change, we will update this page and its effective date and adjust in-app notices and store privacy information as needed. Contact ywhdt_zh@outlook.com.
