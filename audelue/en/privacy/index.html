Audelue Privacy Policy

1. Audio and history2. Pro cloud processing3. Balance and purchases4. Consent and deletion5. Website and support6. Contact and updates

1. Audio and history

Audelue processes selected audio only after you start a session and grant permission. Local mode transcribes and translates on your device without uploading audio or captions. Screen recording permission captures audio only, not video.

The app does not save recordings. Settings stay on your Mac. With history enabled, captions and session information remain until you delete them, without automatic cloud sync.

2. Pro cloud processing

With your consent, your device sends audio, language settings, and keywords directly to Soniox for transcription and optional Real-time translation. Quality translation sends source text, necessary context, and preferences through Audelue’s servers to DeepSeek or OpenAI. If the primary service fails, the other may receive the same text.

Audio bypasses Audelue’s servers; translation text is processed there temporarily, without being saved. Connections are encrypted and servers use Microsoft Azure. Processing may occur outside your country or region. Providers retain received data under their own policies: Soniox · DeepSeek · OpenAI.

3. Balance and purchases

We link your balance using Apple app and purchase verification data. While providing the service, we keep necessary purchase, usage, trial, and device authorization records to restore unused time, handle refunds, and prevent duplicate credits. These records, and service status and error records, exclude audio, captions, and translation preference content.

Apple handles purchases. We receive no payment card details or Apple account email or password. See Apple’s privacy policy for its diagnostics and data handling.

4. Consent and deletion

Withdraw consent in Settings → Cloud & Privacy to stop new cloud processing while keeping history and remaining time. Delete history separately; turning saving off keeps existing records.

Contact us to unlink a device or delete the cloud account. Account deletion ends cloud sessions and removes device links and authorization data. Records needed for balance restoration, refunds, duplicate prevention, and security remain; old backups are removed within seven days.

Uninstalling the app keeps cloud records. Manage exports and device backups separately. Local deletion does not remove data already received by providers; contact us to request deletion.

5. Website and support

The app and website have no advertising or usage analytics, and use no tracking cookies. See GitHub’s privacy statement for the website host’s handling of visit information.

The developer and email provider receive support email addresses, messages, and attachments. These are kept as needed to resolve issues or meet legal obligations. Contact us to request deletion.

6. Contact and updates

Audelue is for users aged 18 and older, developed and operated by ZHANG WENTIAN. For access, correction, deletion, or privacy questions, email wentianzh.apps@outlook.com. Policy updates appear on this page.
