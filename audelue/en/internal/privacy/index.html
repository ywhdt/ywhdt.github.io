Audelue Internal Testing Privacy Policy

This policy covers the Audelue internal testing version distributed through TestFlight.

Local processing requires no registration. With cloud Pro, you do not enter a provider API key. We verify App Store ownership to associate your cloud balance.

1. Audio and local history

Audelue processes your selected system, app or microphone audio only after you start a session and grant the necessary permissions, for transcription and optional translation. Screen recording permission is used to capture audio, not screen video. Apple local mode processes audio and captions on your device without sending them to Audelue’s cloud service.

The app does not save recordings. Settings, keywords and translation preferences stay on your device. With history enabled, source captions, translations and related session information are kept until you delete them, for viewing and export. This history is not automatically synced to the cloud.

2. When you use cloud Pro

This internal TestFlight build sends audio through Audelue’s servers to the selected Soniox or Microsoft MAI service. Real-time translation uses Soniox; text translation uses the selected DeepSeek or Google Gemini service.

Relevant languages, limited preceding context, keywords and translation preferences are also sent to the selected provider. Audelue’s servers hold audio and caption content temporarily for processing and do not save it as server history. Cloud connections use encrypted transport; device authorization credentials are protected by macOS Keychain.

Our cloud infrastructure is hosted by Microsoft Azure. Cloud processing may take place outside your country or region. Model providers process and retain received data under their own policies, separately from Audelue’s retention policy. Please review the relevant information before enabling cloud processing: Soniox · DeepSeek · Microsoft Speech · Google Gemini.

3. Cloud balance and purchases

We use app and purchase verification information provided by Apple to associate your balance. We keep necessary transaction, time usage, once-only trial and device authorization records to restore unused time, handle refunds and prevent duplicate purchase or trial credits. These records do not include audio, captions or translation preference content. Necessary records are retained while we provide these services.

Apple handles purchases. We do not receive payment card details or read your Apple account email or password. Apple may provide diagnostics and feedback according to your system or TestFlight settings. See Apple’s privacy policy.

For service reliability, we process limited failure categories, counts and timings, without audio, captions, prompts or API key content.

4. Your choices and deletion

You can pause or end a session, withdraw consent to future cloud processing, or use Settings to revoke this device’s authorization or delete the cloud account. Withdrawing consent stops new cloud processing and keeps local history and remaining time. Turning history saving off does not delete existing history; individual sessions or all history can be deleted separately.

Deleting the cloud account ends cloud sessions and removes device ownership links and verification materials. Local history is kept. Necessary records for balance restoration, refunds and duplicate prevention, and necessary revocation records, remain. Old backup copies rotate out within seven days.

If you later choose to restore access, we verify App Store ownership again and restore only unused time. Consumed time and previously claimed trial time are not credited again. Ordinary startup and local mode do not automatically restore the cloud account.

Manage exported files and device backups separately. Uninstalling the app does not delete cloud records. Audelue cannot recall data already received by a provider; contact us if you wish to request deletion.

5. Website and support

The app and website contain no advertising or usage analytics. The website uses GitHub Pages without tracking cookies. GitHub may process visit information to provide and protect hosting. See GitHub’s privacy statement.

If you email support, the developer and email provider receive your address, message and attachments to handle the issue. These are kept as needed to resolve the issue or meet legal obligations. You can request deletion of support information.

6. Contact and updates

This policy covers this version, intended for users aged 18 and older. The developer and operator is ZHANG WENTIAN. For privacy questions or requests to access, correct or delete relevant information, contact wentianzh.apps@outlook.com. Policy changes will be posted on this page.
